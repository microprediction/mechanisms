Leads with the experiments (the microprediction platform's chained games: 225-sample nearest-the-pin pool, z1~ PIT streams, z2~/z3~ Morton z-curve copula streams, stacked lottery, monteprediction, MidOne — fact-checked against `microprediction/rediz`, `microconventions`, and the 2020 copula-contest post), then the theory of when a chain is proper, then a verdict on each game's validity (base pool made roughly proper by an accidental jitter for discrete outcomes, the very fix the theory prescribes; z1~ a valid pool but a calibration diagnostic; z2~/z3~ proper elicitation but a curve-distorted metric). | [predictors-as-markets.md](predictors-as-markets.md) | Working draft v0.10 | "Predictors as Markets: One maker, makers in parallel, markets in series". Which predictors are markets, in three movements. One maker: no-arbitrage is a chord condition, not convexity; rational flow trades the biconjugate (contact-set landing, gap pass-through, book holes as multimodal quotes); a proportional fee, conjugated, is exactly a bid-ask spread, and a fee of at least the arbitrage depth restores no-arbitrage. In parallel: combining fee-bearing makers is an infimal convolution solved by one clearing-price root-find with lasso-sparse fills; the aggregate is a consolidated limit order book; self-set fees with routing as the discipline; a deep quadratic co-quoter is Moreau smoothing. In series: one market per chain-rule factor; the market step is the Kalman update, trades are belief-propagation messages (exact on trees), and loopy inconsistency is a sure-arbitrage certificate. Parallel and serial are the two semiring operations of inference. Companion research notes: [proportional-fees-and-the-order-book.md](../research/proportional-fees-and-the-order-book.md), [predictors-as-markets.md](../research/predictors-as-markets.md), [serial-markets-and-belief-propagation.md](../research/serial-markets-and-belief-propagation.md); implemented in [`fee_routing.py`](../mechanisms/fee_routing.py), [`nonconvex_maker.py`](../mechanisms/nonconvex_maker.py), [`serial_markets.py`](../mechanisms/serial_markets.py). |
