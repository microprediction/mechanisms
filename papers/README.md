| [multi-stage-solicitation.md](multi-stage-solicitation.md) | Working draft v0.3 | "Multi-Stage Solicitation of Probability Distributions: Experiments, Theory and Perspective on Conformal Prediction". Leads with the experiments (the microprediction platform's chained games: 225-sample nearest-the-pin pool, z1~ PIT streams, z2~/z3~ Morton z-curve copula streams, stacked lottery, monteprediction, MidOne — fact-checked against `microprediction/rediz`, `microconventions`, and the 2020 copula-contest post), then the theory of when a chain is proper, then a verdict on each game's validity (base pool made roughly proper by an accidental jitter for discrete outcomes, the very fix the theory prescribes; z1~ a valid pool but a calibration diagnostic; z2~/z3~ proper elicitation but a curve-distorted metric). Adds composability as a fifth axis to the log-vs-CRPS debate: when densities are chained, only the log score's per-stage increment is itself the proper score of what the stage added. Population worked example (Gaussian fit to t3), conformal as the limiting case. |
| [non-convex-market-makers.md](non-convex-market-makers.md) | Working draft v0.1 | "Non-Convex Market Makers". Convexity is not what makes a cost-function maker coherent: no-arbitrage is a chord condition, and convexity enters the standard axiomatics through monotone information incorporation instead. Rational flow sees the convex envelope (contact set, gap pass-through); a fee of at least the arbitrage depth restores no-arbitrage and, conjugated, is a bid-ask spread, with the exact no-trade interval given by fee-widened chord bounds; combining fee-bearing makers is an infimal convolution solved by one clearing-price root-find with sparse participation and a consolidated order book; a deep quadratic co-quoter is Moreau smoothing that preserves coherence; and separation turns cross-market inconsistency into a paid certificate. Companion research notes: [proportional-fees-and-the-order-book.md](../research/proportional-fees-and-the-order-book.md), [predictors-as-markets.md](../research/predictors-as-markets.md); implemented in [`fee_routing.py`](../mechanisms/fee_routing.py), [`nonconvex_maker.py`](../mechanisms/nonconvex_maker.py). |
| [clamped-markets.md](clamped-markets.md) | Working draft v0.1 (synthesis) | "Clamped Markets". A synthesis placing known correspondences in one compositional calculus: parallel composition as infimal convolution, serial composition as one venue per chain-rule factor, and the two as one semiring. Its spine is clamping — familiar methods are these mechanisms with a degree of freedom frozen (entry closed gives Bayesian model averaging, capital fixed gives least squares, propagation computed gives the Kalman filter, a single unconditional residual entry gives split-conformal prediction, forfeiting I(R;X)), with nearest-the-pin point-cloud pools at the unclamped end. A synthesis: identities are cited and used, not claimed; the algebra connecting them, and the clamping account, are the contribution. |
