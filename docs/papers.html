Scoring Point-Cloud Distributional Submissions

Nearest-the-pin parimutuels, the KDE seam, and mollified scoring

A parimutuel over a continuum splits the pot in proportion to the density each participant placed at the realised outcome: the nearest-the-pin reward behind monteprediction.com. When the submission is a cloud of samples smoothed into a KDE, scoring at the raw outcome pays the deconvolution of the belief; jittering the pin with the same kernel restores strict propriety, and repeating the repaired score across smoothing scales splits the edge into Fisher-divergence payments.

An Algebra of Prediction-Rewarding Mechanisms

Scoring rules, market makers, and pools as composable transducers

Scoring rules, market makers, parimutuels, and opinion pools compose when every stage consumes and emits one object type, a distributional belief, with wealth threaded as state. Convex potentials generate the incentive-bearing core (Savage's characterisation, Hanson's conjugate market maker, the CFMM level-set duality), the two opinion pools are the two KL barycenters, and merging market makers is infimal convolution. The message type extends to sample clouds via jittered settlement, joint laws factor into margin stages plus a rank-settled copula stage, and stagewise equilibrium dominates conformal prediction: a residual market collects the conformal information gap $I(R;X)$ as bankroll growth while marginal coverage stays exact.
