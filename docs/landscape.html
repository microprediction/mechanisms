The landscape

Deployed contest platforms, prediction markets, and the mechanisms they run, classified by what participants submit, how it settles, whether an aggregation stage exists, and what state threads between rounds. Surveyed July 2026; corrections and additions welcome by PR.

The classification follows the algebra paper: a stage consumes reports, threads a state (wealth, reputation, stake), and emits an aggregate plus transfers. Multi-stage below means one mechanism's output is the priced or scored object of another, not merely a repeated game. Entries marked ·M· have that structure. Statuses with (?) could not be fully verified.

The tote is the oldest margins-and-dependence market structure we know of: marginal and joint pools have run simultaneously on the same races since 1931, and the documented win-versus-exotic arbitrage (Hausch–Ziemba–Rubinstein and successors) is the consistency trade between them.

Where the stages actually chain

Reading the tables for genuine chains, one mechanism's output as another's priced object, and setting aside repetition and mere selection funnels:

  • Residual pricing at scale: the two-settlement electricity market, real-time settling the day-ahead residual, with convergence bidders as the informed entrants; FTRs a further layer on the congestion component.
  • Margins and dependence in separate pools: the racetrack tote since 1931; dispersion trading and (historically) CDO base correlation in derivatives.
  • Markets on forecasts: Polymarket's markets on Nate Silver's model output; Numinous scoring a tournament against Polymarket resolutions; Manifold's self-referential markets (play money).
  • Forecasting the forecasters: Allora's second tier predicts the first tier's losses; Numerai and its relatives pay marginal contribution to an internal meta-model.
  • Distributional chains: the retired microprediction platform (z-streams and dependence streams contested downstream of every stream); monteprediction as the live wealth-threaded pool.

Nothing live combines the last column with the first four: no platform currently chains distributional elicitation into residual or dependence markets with wealth as the routed state. That gap is the subject of the algebra paper's open problems.
